Sound Transit Link Corridors & Property Values

Sound Transit's light rail expansion continues to reshape Pacific Northwest urban geography. Stations along the 1 Line (connecting Sea-Tac Airport, Downtown Seattle, Capitol Hill, University of Washington, and Northgate) consistently show higher price resilience and stronger rental demand.

The upcoming 2 Line East Link connection across Lake Washington to Bellevue and Redmond will further bridge employment corridors, reducing commute friction for tech workers between Seattle and Eastside corporate centers.

Understanding King County Property Taxes

Washington State operates under a budget-based property tax system rather than a fixed rate system. The King County Assessor establishes fair market value annually as of January 1st, while individual taxing districts (schools, fire, city services) establish total budget requirements.

Consequently, a rise in assessed value does not automatically trigger an equal tax increase; taxes only rise if a property's assessed value climbs faster than the average rate of change across the surrounding taxing district.
