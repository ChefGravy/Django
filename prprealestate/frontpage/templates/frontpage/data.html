Infrastructure Corridors

Sound Transit 1 Line & 2 Line Light Rail Arteries

Regional transit expansion remains the paramount long-term driver of Seattle residential liquidity and urban density.

  • • 1 Line North/South Spine: Direct high-speed connection linking Lynnwood, Northgate, the University of Washington, Capitol Hill, Downtown Seattle, and Sea-Tac Airport.
  • • 2 Line East Link Expansion: The historic light rail crossing across the Lake Washington I-90 floating bridge connecting Downtown Seattle, Mercer Island, Downtown Bellevue, and Microsoft campus in Redmond.
  • • Value Premium: Properties within a 10-minute walk of rapid transit stations consistently demonstrate a 7–12% liquidity premium during market contractions.
Zoning & Urban Density

Washington Middle Housing Law (HB 1110)

Enacted in 2023, Washington State House Bill 1110 represents the most consequential structural change to residential land use in decades.

  • • Universal 4-Unit Rights: Tier 1 cities like Seattle must permit at least four residential units on all predominantly single-family lots.
  • • Transit Transit Proximity (6 Units): Lots within 1/4 mile of major transit hubs or light rail stations qualify for up to six units.
  • • Parking Mandate Elimination: Prohibits mandatory off-street parking mandates within 1/2 mile of transit, significantly reducing construction cost burdens.
Fiscal Framework

King County Property Tax Mechanics: The Budget-Based Levy System

Unlike jurisdictions with fixed tax percentages, Washington State calculates property taxes via an aggregate budget-based levy system. Taxing districts (schools, cities, county, port) establish their total annual revenue needs, subject to a 1% statutory growth limit plus new construction. The levy rate is determined by dividing that budget by the total taxable assessed value of all district property.

Levy Rate = (Total Approved District Budget) ÷ (Total Taxable Value in District)

Consequently, an increase in your individual property valuation does not automatically equate to a proportionate increase in your tax bill. Your taxes only increase if your home's appreciation exceeds the average appreciation of other properties within your taxing district.
