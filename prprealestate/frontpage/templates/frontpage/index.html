Understanding King County’s Budget-Based Property Tax Mechanics

A common misconception among home buyers is that an increase in assessed valuation produces an equal percentage increase in property taxes. In Washington State, property taxes operate under a budget-based levy system:

Levy Rate ($/1,000) = (Approved Taxing District Budget) ÷ (Total Taxable Assessed Value in District)
Scenario A: Uniform Growth

If your property's assessed value rises 12% and the district average rises 12%, the overall levy rate adjusts downward. Your tax bill only increases by the district's capped budget increase (~1%).

Scenario B: Outperformance

If your home appreciates 18% while your district averages only 8%, your proportionate share of the total tax burden expands, producing a higher relative tax bill.

Scenario C: Assessment Lag

If your home appreciates 5% while the district average climbs 12%, your actual property tax liability can decline despite an increase in your assessed valuation.
