The Story Behind PRP Real Estate

Growing up in Seattle since the early 1990s gave me a front-row seat to the Puget Sound's incredible evolution. I attended middle school on Queen Anne Hill back when the Space Needle was still surrounded by gravel parking lots and Ballard was still predominantly known for commercial fishing vessels and maritime boatyards.

Over the past three decades, I watched Seattle blossom into one of the world's most dynamic tech capitals. Yet despite all the new glass towers and corporate campuses, each neighborhood maintains its soul: the historic brick of Ballard Ave, the bohemian artistry of Fremont, the sweeping panoramic sunsets from Kerry Park, the coastal ocean air at Alki Beach, and the community warmth along Greenwood Avenue.

I created PRP Real Estate as an homage to the city I love. It is designed to be an objective, thoughtful space where prospective buyers, long-time residents, and curious explorers can learn about Seattle neighborhoods without pushy sales pitches—supplemented with modern AI tools to answer questions in real time.
