Background & Journey

Raised in Seattle since the early 1990s, I have had a front-row seat to the Puget Sound's rapid transformation into one of the world's most dominant technological and economic corridors. From the growth of tech titans like Amazon and Microsoft to the evolution of historic neighborhoods like Queen Anne, Ballard, and Capitol Hill, the Pacific Northwest has always been my home.

I graduated from the University of Washington with a B.A. in Economics and completed my Master of Science in Finance at Seattle University. My academic grounding in capital markets, monetary policy, and econometric modeling taught me to analyze housing not just as emotional real estate, but as a dynamic asset class governed by demographic inflows, transit infrastructure, and macroeconomic supply constraints.

In addition to finance and real estate, I develop full-stack web platforms and autonomous machine learning workflows using Python, Django, Next.js, and neural language models. PRP Real Estate serves as a testbed and showcase for merging clean software design with objective neighborhood intelligence.
