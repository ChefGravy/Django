The Story Behind PRP Real Estate

Growing up in Seattle since the early 1990s gave me a front-row seat to one of the most remarkable regional evolutions in American history. I attended middle school on Queen Anne Hill back when the Space Needle was still flanked by open gravel lots and Ballard was still predominantly known for commercial fishing vessels, maritime shipyards, and Scandinavian dry-dock workers.

Over the subsequent thirty years, I watched the Puget Sound blossom into a global epicenter of technology, commerce, and modern architectural innovation. Yet despite the influx of capital and corporate towers, each neighborhood fiercely maintained its soul: the historic brick masonry and craft culinary culture along Ballard Avenue, the eclectic bohemian artistry lining the Fremont canal, the breathtaking panoramic viewpoints from Kerry Park, the salty ocean promenade at Alki Beach, and the leafy Craftsman bungalows along Greenwood Avenue.

"Real estate is not merely a transaction of physical square footage; it is the physical foundation where family memories are anchored and where substantial capital is either preserved or compromised."

I founded PRP Real Estate to provide an alternative to conventional sales-driven brokerage practices. Drawing on my academic background—a B.A. in Economics from the University of Washington and an M.S. in Finance from Seattle University—I approach every residential acquisition and divestment through an institutional fiduciary lens: analyzing replacement costs, cash flow yields, municipal zoning shifts (HB 1110), and King County property tax mechanics.

Supplemented with our proprietary local neural AI models, clients receive an unvarnished, data-grounded perspective on Seattle real estate—free from sales pressure, backed by three decades of local trust.
